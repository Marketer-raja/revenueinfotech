About RevenueInfoTech

RevenueInfoTech is a technology-news publication covering artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, software, cloud computing, consumer technology and developments across the digital industry.

What we publish

We track announcements, product changes, security advisories, research and other developments that may matter to technology readers. Our coverage should explain what happened, when it happened, who is affected and where readers can verify the information.

How our news workflow works

Technology-news feeds help our editorial desk discover potential stories. A feed item is a research lead, not a finished RevenueInfoTech article. Before an item is selected for the homepage, an editor should open and check the original source, confirm key facts and dates, write an original summary in their own words, add relevant context and attribution, and approve the item in the Newsdesk.

We do not claim that every feed item is independently investigated, that we conduct laboratory tests, or that outside experts review every story. Where we publish original testing or interviews, the article should explain the actual method and participants.

Corrections and feedback

If you notice an error, outdated information or attribution issue, please contact us through our Contact page. Include the relevant page URL and the correction you believe is needed.
