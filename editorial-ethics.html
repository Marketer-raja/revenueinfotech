Editorial Ethics

Last updated: October 17, 2026

RevenueInfoTech is an independent editorial technology publication. Readers rely on us to explain what is happening in technology, so we hold our work to the standards below. They apply to everyone who contributes to the Site.

1. Editorial Independence

Editorial decisions, including what we cover, how we rate products and what conclusions we draw, are made by our editors. Advertisers, sponsors, affiliate partners and vendors do not review, approve or influence our coverage. Our Sponsorship Disclosure explains how we separate commercial activity from editorial work.

2. Accuracy and Verification

  • We check claims against primary sources wherever possible: official documentation, company announcements, filings, release notes, research papers and our own testing.
  • We separate what has been confirmed from what is reported, rumoured or claimed by a vendor, and we say which is which.
  • We state versions, dates and test conditions so readers can judge whether information still applies to them.
  • Headlines and summaries must fairly reflect the content they describe.

2a. Original Content and News Roundups

Some pages on the Site collect headlines from other publications and company newsrooms. On those pages we:

  • name the original source on every item and link directly to it;
  • use only the headline and a short excerpt or summary, never the full article;
  • do not present another outlet's reporting as our own.

Articles we publish under our own name are written or commissioned by us. Where we draw on another outlet's reporting, we credit it.

3. Sourcing and Attribution

We credit the people and organisations whose work informs ours. We do not copy text, images or data from others without permission or a clear legal basis, and we respect the licences attached to images and code samples we use.

4. Use of AI Tools

We may use AI tools to assist with tasks such as research, transcription, summarising or drafting. A human editor is responsible for everything we publish, and checks facts, sources and quotations before publication. We do not publish AI-generated content that has not been reviewed by a person, and we do not use AI to invent quotations, sources, test results or author identities.

5. Conflicts of Interest

  • Contributors disclose financial or personal relationships with companies they cover and may be reassigned if a conflict is material.
  • If a vendor lends hardware or provides software access for review, we say so in the article.
  • We do not accept payment, gifts or other consideration in exchange for coverage or favourable ratings.

6. Corrections and Updates

When we find a mistake, we correct it promptly. Material corrections are noted on the article with the date of the change. Articles that are updated for new information show a revised date, and we explain significant changes. To report an error, please use our Contact page.

Spotted something wrong? Tell us through the Contact page and include the article link and what should change. We review every report.

7. Raising Concerns

If you believe we have fallen short of these standards, contact us through the Contact page. We will review your concern and respond where a reply is appropriate.
