Sponsorship Disclosure

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Publishing costs money. This page explains how RevenueInfoTech earns revenue and the rules we follow so that commercial relationships never shape what we report or recommend.

1. How We Earn Revenue

  • Display advertising. The Site carries ads served by third parties, including Google AdSense. See our Privacy Policy for how advertising cookies work and how to opt out.
  • Sponsored content and sponsorships. We may publish content or newsletter placements paid for by a company. Enquiries go through our Contact page.
  • Affiliate links. Some links in reviews and buying guides may earn us a commission if you buy through them, at no extra cost to you.

2. How We Label Commercial Content

TypeHow it is labelled
AdvertisementsMarked as advertising and kept visibly separate from articles.
Sponsored postsMarked "Sponsored" at the top of the page, naming the sponsor, and written or approved by the sponsor where applicable.
Affiliate linksDisclosed on the page, near the links, and tagged so search engines understand they are commercial.
Loaned or free productsStated in the review, along with whether the product was returned.

3. What Sponsors Cannot Do

  • Pay for editorial coverage, a favourable review or a higher ranking in a comparison or buying guide.
  • Review, approve or change editorial articles before publication.
  • Require us to remove accurate information about their products.

Our editors choose what to cover and how to rate it, using the standards in our Editorial Ethics and Peer Review Process.

4. Our Commitments to Readers

  • Sponsored material is never presented as independent journalism.
  • If a commercial relationship could reasonably affect how you read a piece, we tell you.
  • We follow applicable advertising and endorsement disclosure rules, such as the U.S. FTC Endorsement Guides.

5. Questions

If a commercial relationship is unclear on any page, or you think something is not labelled properly, tell us through the Contact page.
