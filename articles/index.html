Amazon has a plan to index the world’s online shopping data

Amazon is reportedly building a project called World Product Graph, which would gather data on almost every product sold anywhere in the world, including items Amazon does not stock. According to a Reuters exclusive, the aim is to keep shoppers on Amazon.com and give AI shopping agents a reason to start their searches there. Amazon disputes the report's figures on its own assistant, saying they are outdated.
